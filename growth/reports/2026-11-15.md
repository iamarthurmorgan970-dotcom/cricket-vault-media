## What we learned (confidence)

- **Days without a new Short are nearly dead** (fairly sure, 3 gap days): 37, 46 and (partly) 88 views vs a
  200-1,067 range on post days. The channel lives on the latest Short; old ones barely get fed. Since 9 Nov
  the daily task has posted every other day, and that is the likeliest single reason the week fell.
- **Full 5-row cards beat 2-row cards** (early signal, 4 cards, views only). 5 rows: Rabada 1,118 views,
  Akash day 515. 2 rows: Arshdeep 322, Abhishek about 126 in two days. Frame 1 of the Abhishek card is
  two rows in the top half and a big black gap below; the Akash card fills the screen with 5 gold numbers.
- **The hook number should be small and felt** (early signal, 1 video). "11 balls" is instantly shocking;
  "1,024 balls" needs a second of maths, and a 140-ball margin isn't a "no way!".
- **First subscribers** arrived 10-12 Nov, right after the "Follow for more cricket records" pill started
  (9 Nov). Could also be chance or older videos; can't tie subscribers to a video without vidIQ. Promising.
- **Posting time: still no evidence.** 13:00 and 17:30 have 1 post each, and without per-video data even
  those can't be judged.
