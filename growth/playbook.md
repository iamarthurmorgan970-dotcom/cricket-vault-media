# The Cricket Vault: growth playbook

The daily task follows this file when choosing, titling and captioning posts. The weekly review rewrites it
from the channel's real numbers. Keep it short: rules, the evidence for each rule, and what's being tested.

## How the Shorts algorithm judges us (what we optimise)

1. **Stayed rate**: engaged views ÷ views. YouTube counts a "view" from the first frame, so this is the
   share of people who saw the Short in their feed and didn't swipe away. This is the #1 signal.
2. **Average % viewed**: above 80% is good; above 100% means people rewatch (loops).
3. **Engagement per view**: likes, comments, shares, subscribers gained.
4. **Consistency**: one Short a day, at the time our audience is on.

94% of our views come from the Shorts feed (2,882 of 3,074 in 28 days), 6% from search. Winning the feed is the job.

## Current rules

- **The whole point must be readable in frame 1.** No empty charts, zeros or dashes counting up.
  Evidence (3 videos): the three 17-20s videos that build up (thriller, Kohli v Root, Bumrah v Starc)
  kept only 12-13% of feed viewers; the two cards that show the finished ranking at once kept 21% and 30%.
- **Records beat comparisons right now.** Prefer a verified record card (a "first ever / fewest ever /
  most ever" with a ranked list) over an X-vs-Y comparison when both are ready.
  Evidence (early, 2 cards vs 3 others): cards 23% stayed and 100-200% viewed; comparisons/thriller
  13% stayed and 53-77% viewed. Rabada card is our most-viewed Short (1,118 views).
- **Short and loopable wins.** 6-second cards were watched 1-2 times over (Rabada 200%, Arshdeep 101%);
  20-second comparisons were watched 73-77% of the way. If a comparison is posted, its twist number must
  be on screen in the first second (the 3 Nov template does this; Philander is the first test of it).
- **The fact must survive "no way!".** Big, simple gaps or all-time records only. Close calls stay parked.
- **Match Shorts must be fresh** (within a day of the match). The week-old IND v NZ thriller was our
  worst: 12% stayed, 53% viewed, 0 likes (324 views).
- **Every caption ends with a debate question, and the first comment asks a different, pick-a-side
  question** ("Rabada or Steyn?"). Still 0 comments and 0 shares on 3,068 views, so keep pushing it.
- **Titles: put the record or the big number in the first 5 words.** No title pattern has shown a
  difference yet (question vs statement, number first, emoji: all 5 videos have an emoji), so this is
  the house style, not a proven rule.
- **India-only is not required.** The best video was South African (Rabada); the 4 India-player
  videos averaged 488 views. Pick the strongest record, whoever it's about.
