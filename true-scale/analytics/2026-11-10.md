## 5. Choosing today's video

Candidates considered (from the queue): 005 Kedarkantha vs Brahmatal, 006 Lonar crater, 007 No bridge Europe-Africa,
012 Greenland, 025 Roopkund vs Chadar.

**Chosen: 005 Kedarkantha vs Brahmatal (Trek vs Trek #1).**

- **Seasonality (strongest signal):** it's mid-November. Indian winter-trek planning and bookings for the
  December-February snow window happen now. Both treks are sold primarily as winter snow treks (Indiahikes:
  Kedarkantha's busiest window is mid-December to mid-January). A comparison video published now has
  4-8 weeks of rising search demand ahead of it; published in February it would be late.
- **Search intent is proven:** many trek operators (Trek The Himalayas, Bikat, Trekup India, Trekkers of India,
  Himalayan Dream Treks, Moxtain, Banbanjara) publish dedicated "Kedarkantha vs Brahmatal" pages, which they
  only do for queries people actually type. A "vs" title matches that query exactly.
- **News hook:** Uttarakhand's draft trekking rules (ETV Bharat, 25 Jan 2026: operator registration, certified
  guides, insurance + medical certificate for trekkers) give a "before you book" reason to watch. Included as an
  honest caveat, labelled as a draft.
- **Channel test value:** it opens the Trek vs Trek series, the first decision-style (comparison) video. Once
  Studio numbers arrive we can compare it against 004 (India list) and 001-003 (world facts).
- Why not the others today: Lonar and Greenland are evergreen (no reason to rush); Roopkund vs Chadar is better
  in December-January when Chadar news (whether the ice forms) appears.

Applied from learnings (general rules, not yet channel-proven): hook states the most surprising verified
number in the first sentence ("same height, but 400 m more climbing"; "a peak almost twice as high"); title
answers a specific search query; thumbnail = 2 names + "vs" + one bold visual; comment prompt at the end.
