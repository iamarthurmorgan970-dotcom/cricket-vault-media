## 5. Choosing today's video

Candidates considered: 006 Lonar crater, 007 No bridge Europe–Africa, 012 Greenland, 013 Indian states vs
countries, 025 Roopkund vs Chadar.

**Chosen: 012 Why Nobody Lives in Greenland (True Scale world).** Moved ahead of 006-011 in the queue.

- **News hook (strongest signal):** On 3 Nov 2026 Greenland's parliament approved the security deal with the
  US and Denmark (16-0, 7 abstentions); Denmark's parliament debates it later in November (Al Jazeera,
  3 Nov 2026; also The Star, SCMP, Good Morning America). Greenland has been one of the most-searched places
  in world news through 2025-26, so a calm, factual "why is it so empty" explainer rides that curiosity
  without being political.
- **Fits the channel's strongest format on paper:** true size + maps + one big contrast number. It extends
  001 (Greenland vs Africa) with a new question, so it can be linked from 001 via end screen.
- **India hook for the core audience:** "two-thirds the size of India, population smaller than half the
  Narendra Modi Stadium" gives Indian viewers an instant reference.
- **Balance test:** after two India videos (004, 005), this is a world video. Once Studio numbers arrive we
  can compare India vs world topics.
- Why not the others today: Lonar has its own news (rising water submerging temples, IIT Bombay study,
  Bombay HC PIL) and is a strong next pick; Gibraltar tunnel news (Spain funding studies, 2026) is steady,
  not urgent; Roopkund vs Chadar is better in Dec-Jan when Chadar ice news appears.

Applied from learnings (general, not channel-proven): most surprising verified number in the first sentence;
title answers a question people already ask ("why does nobody live in Greenland"); thumbnail = 2-3 huge words
+ one shape; comment prompt at the end.
