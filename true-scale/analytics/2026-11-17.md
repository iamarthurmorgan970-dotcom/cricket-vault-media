## 5. Choosing today's video

Candidates looked at: 007 No bridge Europe-Africa, 040 Har Ki Dun vs Deoriatal-Chandrashila, 013 Indian states vs
countries, 017 How big is Antarctica, 018 Kailasa temple.

**Chosen: 007 Why There's No Bridge Between Europe and Africa (True Scale, world).**

- **Balance:** the last two were India place videos (006, 036) and before that a world news video (012). A world
  "why" question rebalances the test so the first Studio numbers can compare India place vs world fairly.
- **Fresh news hook:** on 5 Nov 2026 Euro Weekly News (citing Europa Sur) reported that Morocco's SNED earmarked
  more than 120 million dirhams (about €11 million) for 2027 tunnel studies, and asked whether Spain should abandon
  the project after this year's Ceuta border tensions. Earlier in 2026: Spain approved €1.73 million for technical
  studies (March), and a three-year Spain-Morocco seismic study was formalised (Dec 2025). The tunnel is regularly in
  the news, so search interest is steady rather than spiky.
- **Evergreen curiosity:** "why is there no bridge between Europe and Africa" is a classic geography question that
  works for browse and search all year, and maps + depth charts suit the channel's style.
- **India reference in the hook:** Atal Setu's sea section (about 16.5 km) is longer than the 13-14 km gap,
  continuing the test of India reference points in world videos (012).
- **First in the queue:** 007 was the first planned item, so no reordering needed.
- Why not the others today: 040 is a second winter-trek comparison only a week after 005, before we know if treks
  work (good candidate for later this month); 013 needs a large batch of verified pairings (better on a quieter day);
  017 Antarctica has no specific hook this week; 018 Kailasa would make three India videos out of the last four.
- One subject: the Strait of Gibraltar crossing (why there's no bridge or tunnel and the current tunnel plan). No
  other plan item merged.

Applied (general, not channel-proven): the most surprising verified contrast in the first sentence (two continents
13-14 km apart, closer than Mumbai's sea bridge is long); a "why" title that names the surprise; thumbnail = 4 huge
words ("ONLY 14 KM / NO BRIDGE?") + one bold visual (the gap drawn on the map); a two-option comment question
(train under the sea or the ferry?).

Accuracy notes: width differs by source (Britannica 13 km, Wikipedia 14.2 km), so the video says "13 to 14 km".
Total project cost estimates differ widely (€15-30 bn; Spanish section alone over €8.5 bn), shown as estimates.
The Punta Paloma-Punta Malabata line is the route reported for the exploratory gallery; pins and lines are marked
approximate. The rail link length is 65 km total with about 28 km under the sea (MWN Nov 2025; 27.7 km in Apr 2026).
