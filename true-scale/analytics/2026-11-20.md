## 5. Choosing today's video

Candidates looked at: 016 Gandikota, 021 Living root bridges, 013 Indian states vs countries, 011 Majuli,
040 Har Ki Dun vs Deoriatal-Chandrashila.

**Chosen: 016 Gandikota: India's Grand Canyon (Why You Should Visit, India).**

- **Balance:** the last four alternated India place (036), world (007), India heritage (018), world (017). An India
  place keeps the mix even while we wait for data.
- **Search demand built into the name:** "Grand Canyon of India" / "India's Grand Canyon" is how people already search
  for and describe Gandikota (district website, Outlook Traveller, Insights IAS, Deccan Chronicle all use the
  nickname). The title borrows a phrase people type, instead of a place name many viewers don't know.
- **The channel's own lens:** instead of a plain travel guide, the hook tests the nickname at true scale (Grand Canyon
  about 446 km long and up to 1,829 m deep vs a gorge about 7 km long and about 90 m high). That's a curiosity gap that
  is honest, and it reuses the "laid on the map" visual that defines True Scale.
- **Season:** Gandikota's best months are October to February (Outlook Traveller); late November is the start of
  the winter-weekend trip season from Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Chennai.
- **News hook (soft):** a ~₹78 crore tourism project (foundation stone Aug 2025), reported plans for a tent city,
  ropeway and glass walkway (Dec 2025), and the January 2026 Gandikota festival with helicopter rides. The script says
  plainly that several of these are plans.
- Why not the others: 021 root bridges is stronger in the monsoon or as a "how they grow" evergreen, so it can wait;
  013 needs a big batch of verified state/country pairs and is better as its own careful run; 011 Majuli and 040
  (second trek comparison) can wait for data on whether place or trek videos work.
- One subject: Gandikota (gorge + fort). The Grand Canyon appears only as the size yardstick; no other plan item merged.

Applied (general, not channel-proven): a verified size contrast in the first 15 seconds (446 km vs about 7 km), a
question title that matches existing search phrasing, an honest twist (Grand Canyon's narrowest point, about 180 m,
is about Gandikota's width), thumbnail with 3 huge words + one bold visual (red gorge with a fort on the rim), and a
two-way comment question (real Grand Canyon or India's?).
