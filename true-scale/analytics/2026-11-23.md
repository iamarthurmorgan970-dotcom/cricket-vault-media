## 5. Choosing today's video

Candidates looked at: 013 Indian states vs countries, 021 Living root bridges, 020 Sandakphu vs Goechala,
011 Majuli, 040 Har Ki Dun vs Deoriatal-Chandrashila.

**Chosen: 013 Every Indian State Compared to a Country (India vs the World, true scale).**

- **Untested series:** "India vs the World (true scale)" is the only planned series with zero episodes so far. It is
  the channel's own name pointed at India, so it is the series most likely to define the channel if it works.
- **Proven format elsewhere (general, not channel data):** "Indian states compared with countries" is a long-running
  map format on map and data sites (Brilliant Maps, GZERO "how many countries fit in India", Visual Capitalist and
  Voronoi "India's states compared with countries" for population). Area versions are evergreen and very shareable
  because every viewer looks for their own state.
- **Comment engine:** 28 states means 28 audiences; the prompt "tell me your state and its country twin" invites a
  personal reply from almost every Indian viewer.
- **Balance:** the last three were India place (016), world true size (017), India heritage (018). A data-driven
  India-vs-world video gives a different type of hook without repeating a place format.
- **Season / news:** none needed; it's evergreen, which suits a Monday when the weekend's travel topics (016) are still
  getting their first views.
- Why not the others: 020 Sandakphu vs Goechala and 040 are second trek comparisons and can wait until 005's numbers
  show whether trek videos work; 021 root bridges is better in monsoon; 011 Majuli needs erosion data I couldn't
  read today (see accuracy notes).
- One subject: India's 28 states by area. Union territories (including Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh) are left out on
  purpose and the video says so. The planned "few population comparisons" were left out: no population source could
  be read this run, and the video points to the population version (023) as the next video instead.

Applied (general, not channel-proven): the single most surprising verified match in the first sentence (UP vs UK,
240,928 vs 242,500 km²), a promise and a payoff ("closest matches at the end", Tripura vs Lebanon, 34 km²), a
countdown structure (#1 to #28) that rewards watching to the end, a thumbnail with 4 huge words ("UP = UK + 27 MORE")
and one bold visual (the UK laid on UP), and a personal comment prompt.

Accuracy notes: Wikipedia could not be fetched in this unattended run (permission prompt timed out), so the figures
come from other sources read today. State areas: StatisticsTimes, "Indian states by area" (source stated: Ministry
of Statistics and Programme Implementation, Selected Socio-Economic Statistics India 2017; India total 3,287,469 km²).
Country total areas: Encyclopaedia Britannica, "List of the total areas of the world's countries, dependencies and
territories" (Cyprus = whole island; Israel excludes the West Bank and Gaza; Serbia excludes Kosovo; Bangladesh includes
river area). Every difference and percentage in the script is arithmetic on those two tables (e.g. 10,486 - 10,452 =
34; 357,596 / 342,239 = 1.045). Map shapes are Natural Earth (India's official boundary), used only as pictures; the
numbers never come from the shapes.
