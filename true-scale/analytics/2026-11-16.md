## 5. Choosing today's video

Candidates looked at: 036 Rann of Kutch + Dholavira, 013 Indian states vs countries, 040 Har Ki Dun vs
Deoriatal-Chandrashila, 031 Spiti, 007 No bridge Europe-Africa.

**Chosen: 036 The Rann of Kutch: India's White Desert and a 5,000-Year-Old City (Why You Should Visit, India).**

- **Season is open right now:** Rann Utsav 2026-27 (the Dhordo tent city) began on 1 Nov 2026 and runs to 7 Mar
  2027 (Outlook Traveller; Republic World, 3 Nov 2026). November-February is when people search "Rann of Kutch",
  "Rann Utsav", "white desert" and plan trips, so this is peak search intent.
- **A dated hook inside the season:** the first full-moon window of the season is 23-25 Nov (Outlook Traveller).
  Publishing this week gives the video a week to be found before that weekend.
- **A second, news-backed hook:** in July 2026 India's Ministry of Culture called Dholavira's 10-sign inscription
  "the world's oldest-known signboard" (Business Today, 12 Jul 2026), which keeps Dholavira in current-affairs
  and exam-prep searches.
- **Balance and testing:** the last three were India trek (005), world/news (012) and India place (006). A second
  India place video in a row is a deliberate test: if place videos beat treks and world videos once numbers arrive,
  this series should get more slots.
- **Visual fit:** a salt-desert colour cycle, sanctuary maps, a to-scale city plan, reservoirs drawn to scale and a
  trade-route map suit the channel's maps-and-charts style.
- Why not the others today: 040 Har Ki Dun vs Chandrashila is also seasonal but a second winter-trek comparison only
  a week after 005, before we know if treks work; 013 states-vs-countries is a strong evergreen that needs a large
  batch of verified pairings (better on a quieter day); 031 Spiti is a summer/road-season topic; 007 has no fresh hook.
- One subject: the Rann of Kutch region (white desert, its wildlife, and Dholavira on Khadir Bet inside it), as
  planned in item 36. No other plan item merged.

Applied (general, not channel-proven): the most surprising verified contrast in the first sentence (a wetland that
turns into a salt desert, with a 5,000-year-old city in the middle); title names the place plus the surprise;
thumbnail = 3 huge words + one bold visual (full moon over cracked salt); "which would you pick?" comment question.

Note on the plan title: the item said "4,000-Year-Old City", but UNESCO dates Dholavira from about 3000 BCE (about
5,000 years ago), so the title now says 5,000.
