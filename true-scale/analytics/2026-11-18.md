## 5. Choosing today's video

Candidates looked at: 008 Savandurga & Madhugiri (monoliths), 018 Kailasa temple, 040 Har Ki Dun vs
Deoriatal-Chandrashila, 013 Indian states vs countries, 021 Living root bridges.

**Chosen: 018 Kailasa: The Temple Carved From One Rock (India's Monoliths & Rock Wonders).**

- **Untested series:** we have made world videos, India places, a trek comparison and a hidden-places opener, but
  nothing from the "Monoliths & Rock Wonders" series. Kailasa is that series' strongest topic, so it gives the series
  a fair first test (008 Savandurga/Madhugiri is lower-demand and best after we know whether the series works).
- **Proven curiosity, all year:** "how was Kailasa carved from one rock" is one of the most widely covered Indian
  heritage mysteries online (many blogs and videos framing it as a mystery in 2026 alone). Evergreen search plus a
  huge, verified number (about 200,000 tonnes removed) suits a hook and a thumbnail.
- **Season:** Ellora's comfortable visiting season is October to March, so travel searches are rising now.
- **News hook:** none specific this week (searched; no fresh Kailasa or Savandurga news), so this one leans on
  evergreen demand rather than news. That makes it a useful contrast with 012/007 (news hooks) once numbers arrive.
- **Balance:** sequence is now India place (036), world (007), India heritage (018).
- Why not the others: 040 is a second trek comparison before 005 has data; 013 needs a big batch of verified
  pairings; 021 root bridges is better closer to the dry-season peak in Dec-Feb and pairs with a Meghalaya
  rainfall angle; 008 as above.
- One subject: the Kailasa temple (Cave 16), with only enough Ellora context to place it. No other item merged.

Applied (general, not channel-proven): biggest verified number in the first sentence; "how big is it really"
comparisons (Parthenon base, ten-storey building, Olympic pools); a clear "estimate vs record" line on the
construction-time question; thumbnail = 4 huge words ("CARVED FROM ONE ROCK") + one bold visual (the temple standing
in its cut-out pit); a two-option comment question (under six years, or generations?).

Accuracy notes: rock removed (about 85,000 m³ / 200,000 tonnes) is an estimate (Wikipedia Ellora Caves; Live History
India). The courtyard is 82 x 46 m at the base (Wikipedia); "almost double the Parthenon" is computed from 82 x 46
(about 3,770 m²) vs the Parthenon's 69.5 x 30.9 m base (about 2,150 m²), so the video says "almost twice". The tower
height is 32.6 m above the court (Wikipedia); ASI gives "nearly 23 m over the plinth" (a different reference point).
The Dhavalikar figure (250 labourers, about 5.5 years) is presented as his estimate. Krishna I's reign is given as
756-773 (Wikipedia) and 756-775 (ASI): the video says "about". The inscription line is a paraphrase, labelled so.
Ellora is closed on Tuesdays (MTDC; one travel blog says Monday, which is Ajanta's closing day). The internal plan
in the animations is simplified and labelled so.
