## 5. Choosing today's video

Candidates: 006 Lonar crater lake, 013 Indian states vs countries, 007 No bridge Europe-Africa, 025 Roopkund vs
Chadar.

**Chosen: 006 Lonar: India's Meteorite Crater Lake (Why You Should Visit, India).**

- **Balance:** the last two were India trek (005) then world/news (012). An India *place* video tests the third
  main format while the series question is open.
- **Live news thread:** Lonar's lake has been rising for several years and has submerged at least six temples
  (incl. Kamalja Devi). The Nagpur bench of the Bombay High Court ordered a PIL in Jan 2026 (LatestLY, 22 Jan
  2026); in Mar 2026 the forest minister told the assembly it had risen 20-25 ft and the state would seek wildlife
  permission to pump water out (ThePrint/PTI, 12 Mar 2026); IIT Bombay experts are sampling it (PTI via
  Careers360, 21 Dec 2025). Not breaking this week, but an ongoing, searchable story with a natural debate
  ("pump it or leave it?") that makes a strong comment prompt.
- **Season:** best visiting window is October to March (Outlook Traveller), so "why you should visit this winter"
  matches what people plan right now.
- **Search-friendly evergreen:** "Lonar lake", "Lonar crater", "meteorite crater in India" and the 2020 pink lake
  are perennial exam-prep and travel searches (dozens of UPSC current-affairs pages cover it).
- **Visual fit:** a crater drawn to true scale, an impact animation, an age timeline and a rising-water gauge all
  suit the channel's maps-and-charts style.
- Why not the others today: 013 states-vs-countries is a strong evergreen but needs a big batch of verified
  pairings (better on a quieter day); 007 has no fresh hook; 025 is better in Dec-Jan when Chadar news appears.

Applied (general, not channel-proven): most surprising verified fact in the first sentence ("a hole almost two
kilometres wide, made by a rock from space... temples disappearing under the water"); title that names the
place plus the surprise; thumbnail = 3 huge words + one bold shape; debate-style comment question.
