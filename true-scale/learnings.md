## How YouTube judges long videos (what we optimise)

1. **Click-through rate (CTR)** from impressions: title + thumbnail. Studio shows it; vidIQ/RSS don't, so ask
   Deepak for CTR when it matters.
2. **Average view duration and % viewed**: the first 30 seconds decide most of it. Strong hook, no slow intro.
3. **Views in the first 24-48 hours** relative to the channel's usual: tells YouTube to test it wider.
4. **Session value**: viewers who watch another of our videos (series, end screens, playlists).
5. **Engagement**: comments (ask a question), likes, shares, subscribers gained per 1,000 views.
