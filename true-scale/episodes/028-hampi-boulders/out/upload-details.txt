DESCRIPTION
Hampi looks as if a giant dropped millions of boulders and walked away. Nobody did. Every one was shaped underground,
inside granite roughly 2.5 billion years old. And around the year 1500, the city built among them is estimated to
have been the second-largest city in the world, after Beijing.

In this video: the legend (Kishkindha, Vali and Sugreeva), then the real geology step by step: magma cooling deep
underground, the joints that crack it into blocks, rainwater rotting the corners into grus, and the rounded
corestones left stacked when the land above wears away. Then Hampi at true scale (the granite's age as one day: the
whole Vijayanagara reign lasts about 0.008 seconds), the empire founded in 1336, the travellers who compared it to
Rome, the Virupaksha gateway and the Vittala stone chariot, the fall after Talikota in 1565, UNESCO, the temples the
ASI dug up in 2026, and how to visit (October to February, Hosapete, Matanga Hill, coracles).

The legend of Vali and Sugreeva, or the geology: which story do you find more amazing? Tell me in the comments.
