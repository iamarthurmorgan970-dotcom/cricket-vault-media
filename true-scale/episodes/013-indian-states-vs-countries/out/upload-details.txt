DESCRIPTION
Uttar Pradesh is almost exactly the size of the United Kingdom: 240,928 km² against 242,500 km², less than 1% apart.
And it isn't even India's biggest state.

In this video we go through all 28 Indian states, from Rajasthan to Goa, and slide a country of almost the same size
onto each one at true size (no stretching). Rajasthan vs the Republic of the Congo and Germany, Madhya Pradesh vs Oman,
Maharashtra vs Italy, Gujarat vs Senegal, Karnataka vs Uruguay, Tamil Nadu vs Nicaragua, West Bengal vs Jordan,
Kerala vs Bhutan, the Northeast vs Israel, Slovenia, Kuwait and Lebanon, and Goa vs Luxembourg. At the end: the six
closest twins, down to one pair just 34 km² apart.
