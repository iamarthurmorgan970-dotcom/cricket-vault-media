Kedarkantha and Brahmatal both top out at about 3,800 m, but one makes you climb roughly 400 m more, and the
other puts you face to face with Trishul, a 7,120 m giant almost twice as high as where you stand.

Planning a winter snow trek in Uttarakhand? Here's a side-by-side comparison of India's two favourite
beginner-friendly snow treks: altitude, how much you actually climb, distance and days, how to get there,
summit day vs ridge walk, the best months for snow, crowds, fitness, Uttarakhand's draft trekking rules,
and a clear "pick this one if..." verdict.
