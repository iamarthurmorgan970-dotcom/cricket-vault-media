DESCRIPTION
Around 200,000 tonnes of basalt were cut away by hand to leave behind one temple. Nothing was built and not one block was carried in.

Kailasa (Cave 16 at Ellora, Maharashtra) was carved from the top of a hill downwards more than 1,200 years ago. In this video: where Ellora is and why it holds 34 caves of three faiths, how the top-down method worked, how big Kailasa really is next to the Parthenon and a ten-storey building, how much rock came out, one archaeologist's estimate of how long it took, who ordered it, what the earliest inscription says, what to look for inside, and how to visit (timings, closed day, best season).
