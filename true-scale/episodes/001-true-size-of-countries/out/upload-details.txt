On most world maps, Greenland looks as big as Africa. In reality, Africa is 14 times bigger.
In this video we drag countries around the globe at their true size: Greenland, Russia, Canada, India,
Japan, Australia, Brazil, Indonesia, Alaska and Antarctica. We also explain why the 1569 Mercator map
stretches everything near the poles.
