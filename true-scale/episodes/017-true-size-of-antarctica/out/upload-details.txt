DESCRIPTION
On most maps Antarctica is a thin white smear along the bottom. In reality it is bigger than India and Australia put together, with room left over for almost another India.

In this video: why the Mercator map turns Antarctica into a white band, its real area (about 14.2 million km²), India and Australia dropped onto it at true size, how thick the ice is (about 1.9 km on average, about 4,757 m at its thickest), how much water it holds (about 70% of Earth's fresh water, about 58 m of sea level), Lake Vostok hidden 4 km under the ice, the coldest temperatures ever measured, why it is a desert, the 2026 sea-ice peak (third lowest on record), who lives there, India's stations (Dakshin Gangotri, Maitri, Bharati), and who owns it.
