DESCRIPTION
Greenland is about two-thirds the size of India, yet its whole population (56,740 on 1 January 2026) would fill
less than half of Ahmedabad's Narendra Modi Stadium. So why does almost nobody live on the world's biggest island?

Greenland is in the news after its parliament approved a security deal with the US and Denmark on 3 Nov 2026.
Here's the place behind the headlines, at its true scale: the ice sheet that covers about 80% of the island and
is more than 3 km thick, the cold even in the capital Nuuk, why there are no roads between towns, 4,500 years
of people living with the ice (Saqqaq, Dorset, Thule Inuit, the Norse who vanished), Denmark's role, an economy
built on fishing, and why the world suddenly cares.

Would you live in Greenland for a year if someone paid your rent? Tell me in the comments.
