DESCRIPTION
People call Gandikota in Andhra Pradesh "India's Grand Canyon". The real Grand Canyon is about 446 km long. The
gorge at Gandikota is about 7. So is the nickname fair?

In this video we put the two side by side at true scale: the Grand Canyon laid on the map starting at Gandikota (it
runs right across Andhra Pradesh and about 250 km out into the Bay of Bengal), depth with two Burj Khalifas for
scale, rim-to-rim width, and the twist: the Grand Canyon's narrowest point is about Gandikota's width. Then the
gorge fort itself: the 1123 sand fort, the Kakatiyas and the Pemmasani Nayaks, Mir Jumla and the Jama Masjid,
Tavernier and the diamond trade, the temples and granary inside the walls, the new tourism projects and the January
2026 festival, how to get there and the best months to go (October to February).

Which would you rather see first: the real Grand Canyon or India's? Tell me in the comments.
